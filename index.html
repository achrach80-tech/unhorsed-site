Support

Something broke, a rider's name is offensive, or you want your data deleted? Write to ashraf.rach@gmail.com with your knight name and your phone model. We answer within a few days.

Common questions

  • I changed phones. How do I keep my knight? On the old phone, sign in from the title screen (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone). Sign in with the same account on the new phone and your progress comes back.
  • I bought diamonds but didn't get them. Open Settings (the gear icon) → Restore Purchases. If they still don't appear, e-mail us your knight name and the order number from your store receipt.
  • How do I start over? Settings → Reset Progress erases all saves on the device.
  • How do I delete my data? E-mail us your knight name; we delete your online save, ghosts, ladder line and sign-in within 30 days.
  • Does the game need an internet connection? No. The campaign plays offline; ghost duels and the ladder refresh when you are online.
